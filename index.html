Night Shift turns saturated red and red-orange pink on iPhone 18 Pro

On my iPhone 18 Pro, saturated red and red-orange turn pink with Night Shift on. I first noticed it in photos, where egg yolk looked pink. My iPhone 15 Pro does not show this shift. Both run iOS 27 with Night Shift at More Warm. A Reddit user reports a similar pinkish hue on their 18 Pro compared with an M4 iPad Pro.

Columns show hue. Rows show saturation at HSV value 100%, in Display P3 and sRGB.

  1. Open Settings › Display & Brightness › Night Shift
  2. Drag the Color Temperature slider to More Warm.
  3. Return to this page. In Control Center, touch and hold the brightness slider, then tap Night Shift off and on. Watch the 0° column and the two S100 rows. The shift is strongest in P3 S100.
